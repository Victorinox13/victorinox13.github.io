Help and information for Cap Clash

What is Cap Clash?

Cap Clash is a multiplayer party game where you flick bottle caps to score points. Play quick rounds with friends — 2 or 4 players on one phone, or link multiple phones for bigger matches.

Cap Clash is een multiplayer partygame waarin je met flessendoppen flikt om punten te scoren. Speel snelle rondes met vrienden — 2 of 4 spelers op één telefoon, of koppel meerdere telefoons.

How to play

  1. Start a new game and choose 2-player or 4-player mode.
  2. Same device: pass the phone between turns. Each player flicks their cap by swiping on the screen.
  3. Linked devices: one player hosts the game; others join on their own phone. The host controls the game flow.
  4. Aim for the target zone to score. The player with the most points after all rounds wins.

Start een nieuw spel en kies 2 of 4 spelers. Op één toestel geef je de telefoon door per beurt. Bij gekoppelde telefoons maakt één speler het spel aan (host) en sluiten anderen aan. Mik op de doelzone om te scoren — de speler met de meeste punten wint.

Crowns, skins & purchases

You can earn crowns by winning matches. Crowns and in-app purchases unlock cosmetic cap skins. All purchasable content is cosmetic only and does not affect gameplay.

Note: in-app purchases and the in-game store may not be available in all versions. Cosmetic items are tied to your device.

Troubleshooting

  • App won't open or crashes: make sure you're running the latest version of Cap Clash and iOS. Try restarting the app or your device.
  • Linked play not working: ensure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network or have Bluetooth enabled. Restart the game session if the connection drops.
  • Missing purchases: go to Settings → App Store → restore purchases, or reinstall the app and sign in with the same Apple ID.
  • Other issues: try deleting and reinstalling Cap Clash. Your Apple ID purchases will be restored automatically.

Controleer of je de laatste versie van Cap Clash en iOS gebruikt. Herstart de app of je toestel bij problemen. Voor gekoppeld spelen: zorg dat beide toestellen op hetzelfde Wi-Fi zitten of Bluetooth aan hebben.
