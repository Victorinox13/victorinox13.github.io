Privacy Policy

Cap Clash, the multiplayer party game

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Who we are

Cap Clash is published by Victor Deleeck (Belgium). This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

What we collect

Cap Clash collects only the information necessary to run the game. We do not sell your personal data.

When features such as ads (e.g. AdMob), analytics, or in-app purchases are enabled, the app may collect or share limited data as required by those services. Examples of data categories that may apply include:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID) — for ad delivery, when ads are enabled
  • Purchase history — processed by Apple for in-app purchases
  • Basic usage data — for analytics, when enabled
  • Network information — for device-to-device multiplayer functionality

The exact data practices will be reflected accurately in the App Store Connect App Privacy details (nutrition labels) once the app is published. If any data category changes, we will update both the App Store labels and this policy.

Third-party services

Cap Clash may integrate third-party services such as Apple Game Center, ad networks, or analytics tools. Each of these services has its own privacy policy. When such integrations are active, their data practices apply alongside this policy.

Multiplayer & networking

Cap Clash supports local multiplayer (same device) and linked-device play. Linked-device play may use local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth to connect devices. We do not collect or store the content of multiplayer sessions.

Children's privacy

Cap Clash is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app follows the age rating set in the App Store.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have rights under the GDPR including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since Cap Clash is designed to collect minimal data, most gameplay data stays on your device. For any data-related requests, please contact us at victor@deleeck.be.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at victor@deleeck.be or visit the Cap Clash support page.
