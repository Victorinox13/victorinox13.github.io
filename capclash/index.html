CAPCLASH

Flick caps. Beat your friends.

A party game for the table. Two teams flick bottle caps across the mat onto each other's fields. Play on one phone, or lay up to four phones end to end.

How to play

  • Two teams

    Two teams of two, one at each end. Your side has your team's colour.

  • Who goes first?

    Tap the corner that starts. The order stays the same all game.

  • Shooting

    Swipe or drag forward from your cap; the line shows where it goes. Switch style with the blue or orange dot in your corner.

  • Moving your cap

    Hold your cap, then drag it within your corner.

  • Scoring

    Caps on the other team's fields score 1, 2 or 3. Your score shows at your end.

  • BOSS HIT!

    Land a cap on the BOSS field between the other team's posts.

  • Joker

    After a round: shake your phone. Once per team per game.

  • ALL IN!

    Three rounds in a row without a cap in a field.

  • REFEREE ROUND!

    A cap stops on the CAP CLASH logo.

  • Knocked away?

    One shot per cap in your corner. None left? Skip your turn.

  • Chaos mode

    Table trouble for 1–3 rounds: spills, a cat, a fold in the mat, wind and more.

One phone or the whole table

Pass one phone around, or lay 2, 3 or 4 phones in a line, the bottom of one against the top of the next. The mat runs across all the screens.

Playing on one phone stays free. With the Party Pass you host games on 2, 3 or 4 phones, and anyone who joins plays free.
